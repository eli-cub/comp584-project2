More than just the NHL

Before the rink

My checklist: skates sharpened, stick taped, water filled, and enough time to warm up before getting on the ice.

WHY I PLAY

Nothing beats getting back on the ice

Hockey is one of those sports where you can always find something new to improve. One day it's skating, the next day it's passing or learning how to read a play better.

I enjoy how fast everything happens. Even during a casual pickup game, you have to think, react, and move at the same time.

THE BEST PART OF THE WEEK

Weekend hockey mornings

Some people like sleeping in on the weekend. I'd rather be at the rink. There's something about getting an early skate in, working on your game, and hanging out with the people you play with. It's a great way to start the day.

Skills I'm working on

Stickhandling

Quick hands make it easier to keep possession in traffic. I like short drills where I can get a lot of touches.

Skating and edge control

Skating makes a difference in every part of hockey. Better edge control helps with tighter turns, quicker acceleration, and feeling more confident when changing direction.

Shooting

Getting a shot off quickly matters more than just shooting hard. Accuracy and a fast release are what I want to keep improving.
